# Product quality and safety checklist

Run before the print order, and again on delivered stock. Keep the completed sheet, dated —
it is what you point at when a reviewer asks whether the product is safe and well made.

## Materials and safety

- [ ] Inks declared suitable for children's products; declarations obtained in writing
- [ ] Adhesive used in the padding declared non-toxic
- [ ] No sharp edges after trimming; corners checked on a delivered sample
- [ ] No small detachable parts in the base SKU
- [ ] **If the gift bundle adds pencils, crayons or stickers**: these are separate products with
      their own conformity obligations. Confirm SASO / SABER requirements and obtain supplier
      conformity documentation **before importing or bundling** — see
      `05-operations/compliance-checklist.md`. Bundling an uncertified item is the single most
      likely way this product gets stopped, and it happens after you have paid for it.
- [ ] Packaging carries no suffocation hazard; polybag warning applied where required
- [ ] Age marking on pack consistent with the 5–8 positioning everywhere else

## Print quality — on delivered stock, not the proof

Sample 10 units from different points in the delivery.

- [ ] Black density consistent across all 10
- [ ] No visible banding, streaking or roller marks
- [ ] Registration correct on the 4/0 cover
- [ ] Trim within tolerance; 12 mm safe margins intact on every sheet
- [ ] Reverse of every sheet clean — no set-off, no scuffing
- [ ] Tear-out clean on a full pad, all 20 sheets
- [ ] Binding holds; no sheets loose in the pack
- [ ] Cover lamination intact, no lifting at the edges

## The marker test — repeat on delivered stock

The proof passing is not the run passing. Different paper batches behave differently.

- [ ] Felt-tip marker, hard pressure, full sheet → no ink visible on the reverse
- [ ] Wax crayon → no tearing or pilling
- [ ] Colored pencil → color sits evenly, no shine or skid
- [ ] Water-based paint (a child will try it) → note the result honestly and say so on the PDP
      if the paper does not take it. Telling parents what the product *isn't* for buys more
      trust than it costs in sales.

## Content — final check against the sign-off log

- [ ] All 20 sheets signed off by the cultural/editorial reviewer, by number
- [ ] Every Arabic line proofread on the **physical** proof for shaping and diacritics
- [ ] Difficulty icons present and correct on all 20
- [ ] Talk-together prompts on exactly sheets 2, 6, 9, 13, 15, 16, 19
- [ ] Sheet numbers correct and in order
- [ ] No flag, emblem, official mark or endorsement-implying arrangement anywhere
- [ ] Completion certificate present and correctly worded
- [ ] Every claim on cover and pack checked against `00-strategy/claim-discipline.md`
- [ ] Legal deposit / ISBN number printed where required

## Packaging and unboxing

- [ ] Pack survives a 1 m drop test in its shipping configuration
- [ ] Product arrives flat, uncreased, corners intact
- [ ] Opening it feels deliberate — this is the giftability the price premium rests on
- [ ] Insert card carries the correction-protocol contact and the returns policy
- [ ] Nothing in the pack makes a claim the product cannot support
