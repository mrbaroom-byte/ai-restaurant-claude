# Ad creative briefs

Structured to the brief's acquisition sequence: **Discover → Consider → Trust → Share.**
Each stage has a different job and a different measurable signal. A creative that tries to do
all four does none of them.

**Channel hypothesis:** lead with Instagram and Snapchat; use TikTok selectively for
demonstrative user content. Treat platform reach figures as advertising-reach estimates, not
parent counts — they are not audience sizes and should never be used to forecast demand.

---

## Stage 1 · Discover — the 15–25 second child-use video

**The single most important asset in the launch.** It carries the whole proposition without a
word of copy, and it is also the cheapest thing here to shoot badly.

**Structure:**

| Time | What is on screen | Why |
|------|-------------------|-----|
| 0–2s | A hand switching a tablet off, screen going dark | The tension, stated in one gesture, with no anti-device message |
| 2–4s | A blank sheet being torn from the pad and laid flat | The format, shown not told |
| 4–14s | Real-time coloring, hands only, natural sound, one continuous shot | Proof it is real and proof it holds attention |
| 14–20s | Time-lapse to the finished sheet | The satisfaction, compressed |
| 20–25s | The child holding the finished page up; cut to the pad with the sheet count | Pride, then product |

**Rules:**

- **Shoot a real child using a real printed prototype.** Nothing here works with a render, a
  stock clip, or an adult's hands pretending. Parents can tell instantly, and the trust cost is
  permanent.
- **Hands and the page, not faces.** Child privacy, plus it lets any parent picture their own
  child. Where a face appears, you need a separate signed release for that specific use.
- **Natural sound.** Pencil on paper is more persuasive than a music bed.
- **Designed for sound-off.** Most views have no audio. Everything must read silently.
- **Vertical 9:16**, with the action in the middle third so the platform's UI never crops it.
- **No text overlay in the first 2 seconds** — the picture does the hooking.

**CTA:** شاهد الأوراق العشرين · See the 20 sheets
**Primary signal:** 3-second hook rate, hold rate to 15s, product-page visits.

**Variants to test:** the tablet-off open vs. opening on the blank sheet; ending on the finished
artwork vs. on the pad. Do not test more than one variable at a time.

## Stage 2 · Consider — the four-card carousel

For parents who visited but did not add to cart. Each card answers one objection from survey D1,
in the order D2 ranked them.

| Card | Shows | AR | EN |
|------|-------|----|----|
| 1 | Difficulty icons on three sheets side by side | يناسب أعمار ٥–٨ | Made for ages 5–8 |
| 2 | Close-up of a discovery prompt on a colored sheet | سؤال اكتشاف على كل ورقة | A discovery prompt on every sheet |
| 3 | Four finished sheets showing the range of subjects | من الطبيعة إلى الفضاء | From nature to space |
| 4 | Macro shot of paper edge and thickness, marker test visible | ورق ١٤٠–١٦٠ غم، وجه واحد | 140–160 gsm, single-sided |

**Card 4 is the one that sells.** It is the closest thing to touching the product, and the paper
is what the price premium rests on. Show the *back* of a marker-colored sheet — clean.

**CTA:** اختر دفترك أو الطقم · Choose the pad or the bundle
**Primary signal:** add-to-cart rate, FAQ engagement.

## Stage 3 · Trust — the prototype flip-through

Static feed and product-page embed. Not an ad so much as evidence.

- One unbroken shot, hands turning every one of the 20 sheets at a readable pace
- A hand for scale at least twice
- One tear-out, shown fully
- No cuts, no speed-ramp, no music. **A cut in a flip-through reads as something hidden.**

Pair with the founder's own statement to camera — who made this, who reviewed it, what happens
if there is a mistake. Read the correction promise out loud. A named person taking
responsibility is worth more than any production value you could buy with the same money.

**CTA:** احجز نسختك · Preorder securely
**Primary signal:** checkout completion.

---

## Stage 4 · Share — the finished-artwork gallery

Post-purchase, not paid. This is the engine that makes the economics work, because a customer
who returns costs nothing to acquire — and `03-economics/` shows the model is short of exactly
that.

- The completion certificate photographed with a finished sheet
- Sheet 20 (*My Saudi Arabia*) filled in — the most photographable page in the pad, designed so
  a phone photo needs no cropping
- A gallery on the site collecting what children made

**Consent, every time.** Written parental permission for that specific use, no faces, no names,
no schools. A refusal must never affect the customer's experience.

**Primary signal:** user-generated content rate, review rate, repeat purchase.

## Ten angles to test, ranked by hypothesis strength

The brief calls for message testing before spend. These are the copy angles worth putting behind
the Stage 1 video, strongest hypothesis first:

1. **Creative time that teaches** — the recommended lead (message Cell A)
2. **Twenty sheets, one satisfying session at a time** — the format as the benefit
3. **A better answer to "I'm bored"** — the relief angle (Cell C)
4. **The back stays clean, so it goes on the wall** — the single-sided detail as a feature
5. **Discover Saudi Arabia, one color at a time** — the culture lead (Cell B)
6. **Arabic first, not translated** — the bilingual quality signal
7. **What will your child add?** — the open-ended prompt as the differentiator
8. **From the oryx to outer space** — range, answering "is it all heritage?"
9. **Reviewed by a person we name** — the trust angle as a hook
10. **Made for curious hands** — the pure emotional close

Test one variable at a time. Two creatives differing in headline *and* image tell you nothing
about either.

## What must never appear in any ad

Carried from `00-strategy/claim-discipline.md`, because ad creative is where these slip in:

- Measurable learning-outcome claims — no skills gained, no reading levels, no milestones
- Screen-time guilt, fear, or anti-device framing
- Any flag, state emblem, official mark, or arrangement implying endorsement
- Fabricated social proof — review counts, "sold out", stock counters, fake urgency
- A named institution as a partner or approver without written agreement
- A child's face, name or school without a signed release for that specific use

---

## Budget discipline

The SAR 2,500 paid line is **research spend**, not customer acquisition. Its job is to answer
which message earns qualified intent and what a click actually costs — read
`03-economics/README.md` before assuming paid traffic can be the launch channel at all. It
currently cannot, at the seeded assumptions, and that finding shapes the whole plan: the launch
leans on creator seeding, the founder preorder and organic demonstration.

Spend it to learn. Do not chase a return on it.
