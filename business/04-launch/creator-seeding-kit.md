# Creator seeding kit — Nov 1–10

Ten days, before the 15 November public launch. The purpose is **demonstration and first
reviews**, not reach. A creator whose audience is 40,000 parents in Riyadh who actually films
their child using the pad is worth more than one with ten times the followers reading a script.

Given what `03-economics/` says about paid acquisition, this is not a supporting channel. It is
the primary one.

---

## Who to approach

| Fit | Signal | Avoid |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Saudi parents who post about children's activities | Their own children appear in their normal content | Accounts that post only paid product placements |
| Teachers and early-years educators | They explain *why* something works | Anyone whose comments are visibly inauthentic |
| Arabic-first creators | Their audience reads the language the product is written in | Reach without relevance |
| Riyadh and Jeddah | Matches the paid test's two audiences | Non-Saudi audiences at this stage |

**8–12 creators.** More than that and you cannot brief or support them properly in ten days.

---

## What to send

1. The pad — the **real production unit**, not a prototype
2. A short handwritten note. Name the child if they have mentioned one; this is a person-to-person
   ask, not a mailout
3. A one-page card: what it is, ages, what is in it, who reviewed the content, and the
   correction promise
4. Their discount code, and a code for their audience
5. Nothing else. No script, no shot list, no required hashtags

---

## The brief you actually send

> We made a 20-sheet creative pad for children aged 5–8, inspired by Saudi Arabia. Every sheet
> has a short discovery prompt in Arabic and an open creative invitation.
>
> **What we would love:** film your child using it, honestly. If they love one page and ignore
> another, show that. If it does not hold their attention, tell us — we would rather know.
>
> **What we ask:** please label the post as an ad, in Arabic, in the post itself. Not only in
> your bio or the comments.
>
> **What we will not ask for:** a script, a required caption, approval over what you say, or a
> promise to post at all. If it is not right for your audience, keep it and tell us why.

That last paragraph is the whole strategy. A creator who is free to be critical produces content
parents believe, and parent belief is the thing the product page cannot manufacture.

---

## Disclosure — not optional

- Every gifted or paid post is labelled as an ad **in the post, in Arabic**
- Get the disclosure commitment in writing before you ship
- Never present a creator post as an organic customer review
- Never edit a creator's wording; you may translate it if labelled as translated
- Keep the written brief for every creator on file

See `00-strategy/claim-discipline.md`. Getting this wrong once, publicly, costs more than every
creator fee combined.

---

## Children in creator content

You do not control a creator's own family content, and you should not try to. What you can do:

- Never re-share a post showing a child's face without written permission for **your** use of it
- Never re-share content identifying a child's school or full name
- Say in the brief that you are happy with hands-only footage if they prefer it

## Measuring it

| Signal | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Posts published / kits sent | Whether the product is worth talking about unprompted |
| Comments asking "where can I buy this" | Genuine pull, the strongest signal on this list |
| Saves and shares | Parents sending it to other parents — the mechanism that makes this channel work |
| Code redemptions | Conversion, but expect it to lag; parents save and buy later |
| Unsolicited criticism | The most useful output. Fix it before 15 November. |

**Do not judge this on redemption alone.** Ten days is short and parents deliberate on children's
purchases. Comments and saves lead sales here by weeks.

---

## The founder preorder runs alongside

Nov 1–10 is also the founder preorder window (SAR 39). Two jobs, one period:

1. **First verified-photo reviews** — irreplaceable, and impossible to acquire later at any price
2. **The 20-order operational dry run** — Gate 6. Pack, ship, deliver, handle one return, answer
   one support question. See `05-operations/launch-runbook.md`.

Cap the founder quantity and **write the end date on the page before it goes live.** The failure
mode is that the founder price quietly becomes the launch price because nobody decided to end
it, and `03-economics/` shows the core pad at SAR 39 clears under SAR 6 per order before any
acquisition cost at all.

---

## Ten-day sequence

```
Nov 1     Ship all creator kits; open founder preorder
Nov 2-3   Personal follow-up — a message, not a reminder email
Nov 4-7   Posts begin; answer every comment yourself, in Arabic
Nov 5-8   Twenty founder orders shipped; run the Gate 6 dry run
Nov 8-9   Collect verified-photo reviews; fix whatever the dry run exposed
Nov 10    Founder window closes. Go / no-go on the 15 Nov launch.
Nov 15    Public launch — controlled release, replenish only on evidence
```

If the dry run fails, **delay the launch.** The first fifty reviews of a new brand cannot be
bought back, and a November launch that ships badly is worth less than a December launch that
ships well.
