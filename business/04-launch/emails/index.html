Transactional email set

7 emails covering the founder preorder through the first review request. Arabic-first, table layout, inline styles, no webfonts — built for email clients rather than browsers.

Not ready to send. Merge tags are {{double_brace}} and must be mapped to your platform's syntax, and the CR number, contact and policy URLs are still placeholders. See README.md for the mapping table and the send rules.

Preorder confirmation

01-preorder-confirmation

Subject

Preheader
لم يُخصم أي مبلغ الآن. سنراسلك حين تصبح النسخ جاهزة.
When
Immediately on preorder. Must fire within one minute — a parent messaging to ask whether the order went through is the Gate 6 failure signal.
