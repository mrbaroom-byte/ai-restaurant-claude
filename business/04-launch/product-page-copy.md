| Proof bar | Remove uncertainty | أعمار ٥–٨ · ورق فاخر بوجه واحد · أسئلة ثنائية اللغة، العربية أولًا · أوراق قابلة للفصل والتعليق | Ages 5–8 · single-sided premium paper · Arabic-first bilingual prompts · tear-out display pages |
| CTA | Low-friction next step | شاهد الأوراق العشرين | Explore all 20 sheets |
| Emotional close | Pride without heaviness | صُنع لأيدٍ فضولية. متجذّر في الوطن. | Made for curious hands. Rooted in home. |

**The CTA is not "Buy now" on purpose.** The brief's acquisition sequence puts a low-friction
step between discovery and checkout, because a parent who has not yet seen the sheets has no
basis to buy. "Explore all 20" converts curiosity into the scroll where the trust proof lives.

> **AR** — عشرون ورقة تلوين فاخرة تأخذ طفلك في رحلة عبر السعودية: من المها العربي وواحة الأحساء، إلى رواشين البلد ونقوش القط العسيري، وصولًا إلى الرياض اليوم ورحلة نحو الفضاء.

>
> الأوراق بوجه واحد وقابلة للفصل، حتى يعلّق طفلك ما أنجزه.

> **EN** — Twenty premium coloring sheets that take your child on a journey through Saudi
> Arabia: from the Arabian oryx and the Al-Ahsa oasis, to the carved windows of Al-Balad and the
> patterns of Al-Qatt Al-Asiri, to Riyadh today and a journey toward space.
>
> Every sheet carries three layers: a short discovery prompt in Arabic first, an open-ended
> creative invitation so your child adds something of their own, and — on seven sheets — a small
> question to talk about together.
>
> Sheets are single-sided and tear out, so your child can display what they finished.

## Specifications — state them, don't describe them

The brief's finding is that vague copy and render-only imagery are what kill online trust in
this category. Specificity is the trust mechanism, so this block is deliberately dry.

| | AR | EN |
|---|---|---|
| Age | أساسًا ٥–٨ سنوات | Primarily ages 5–8 |
| Contents | ٢٠ ورقة + شهادة إتمام | 20 sheets + a completion certificate |
| Size | A4 — ٢١٠ × ٢٩٧ مم | A4 — 210 × 297 mm |
| Paper | ورق غير مصقول، ١٤٠–١٦٠ غم | Uncoated, 140–160 gsm |
| Printing | وجه واحد — الخلف يبقى نظيفًا للتعليق | Single-sided — the back stays clean for display |
| Binding | مُلصق من الأعلى، تُفصل الورقة بسهولة | Padded, clean single-sheet tear-out |
| Language | عربي أولًا، مع الإنجليزية | Arabic first, with English |
| Session | ١٥–٢٠ دقيقة تقريبًا للورقة | Roughly 15–20 minutes per sheet |
| Suits | أقلام خشبية، شمعية، ولبادية | Colored pencils, wax crayons, felt-tip markers |

> **Fill "session length" from the play-test data, not from hope.** It is the median minutes to
> first disengagement across 15 children, and it is the most checkable claim on the page.

## The six trust blocks

Ordered by the objection sizing from survey D1 and D2 — put the biggest fear first, not the
easiest answer first.

**1 · Is it right for my child?**
أساسًا لأعمار ٥–٨. كل ورقة تحمل أيقونة صعوبة (● سهل، ●● متوسط، ●●● مفصّل)، والجلسة الواحدة تستغرق ١٥–٢٠ دقيقة تقريبًا. أغلب الأطفال في هذا العمر يكملون ورقة بمفردهم.
*Primarily 5–8. Every sheet carries a difficulty icon; one session runs roughly 15–20 minutes. Most children this age complete a sheet independently.*

**2 · Will it look like the photos?**
فيديو تصفّح كامل، وصورة قريبة للورق، وصورة بحجم اليد — من نموذج مطبوع حقيقي.
*Full flip-through video, macro paper shot, scale-in-hand photo — from a real printed prototype, never renders alone.*

**3 · Are the Arabic and the culture accurate?**
كل ورقة راجعها مختص تحرير ومراجعة ثقافية مستقلة، ونعلن اسمه. وإذا وجدت خطأ، نصححه ونعيد طباعة الورقة ونرسلها لكل من اشترى — مجانًا.
*Every sheet reviewed by a named independent editorial and cultural reviewer. Find an error and we correct it, reprint the sheet, and send it free to everyone who bought that run.*

**4 · Is this seller reliable?**
رقم السجل التجاري ووسيلة تواصل مباشرة، مع مدة الشحن وسياسة الإرجاع مكتوبة بوضوح.
*Commercial registration and a direct contact, with the shipping window and return policy stated plainly.*

**5 · Is checkout safe and convenient?**
دفع آمن عبر مدى والبطاقات، ورسوم الشحن معلنة قبل الدفع.
*Secure checkout with mada and cards, shipping shown before payment.*

**6 · Do other parents trust it?**
Pre-launch, this block is the honesty statement, not a review count:
لم نبِع بعد، فليست لدينا تقييمات بعد. ما لدينا: نموذج مطبوع جرّبه أطفال حقيقيون، ومراجعة ثقافية مستقلة.
*We haven't sold yet, so we have no reviews yet. What we have: a printed prototype tested with real children, and an independent cultural review.*

Post-launch it becomes verified-photo reviews only, published as written, with any sponsored
content labelled as an ad. See `00-strategy/claim-discipline.md`.

## FAQ — ordered by survey D2

1. How is this different from an ordinary coloring book?
2. Will it suit a 4- or 9-year-old?
3. Will markers bleed through?  ← **answer this honestly from the test data**
4. Are pencils included?
5. When will it arrive?
6. What if I don't like it?
7. Who made this, and who checked the content?

> Question 3 will be asked, and the paper test in `02-product/quality-checklist.md` gives you the
> real answer. If water-based paint does not work on this stock, **say so**. Telling parents what
> the product is not for buys more trust than it costs in sales, and it prevents the return.

## Words to use, and words to avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|-----|-------|-----|
| يدعم / supports, encourages, designed for | يحسّن / improves, boosts, guarantees | Unsupported outcome claims — see claim discipline |
| ١٤٠–١٦٠ غم، وجه واحد | فاخر، بجودة عالية | Specifics build trust; adjectives are what every competitor already says |
| بعيد عن الشاشة / screen-light | مضاد للشاشات / anti-screen | The brief bans guilt and anti-device messaging |
| أعمار ٥–٨ | لكل الأعمار | The too-broad age claim is a named top-five risk |
| مستوحى من / inspired by | رسمي، معتمد / official, approved | Never imply endorsement |

## Rewrite this after Sept 18

Everything above is a draft written before a single parent was interviewed. The language bank
gives you:

- the words parents actually use for the useful-vs-filler boundary → rewrites the hero
- the proof they demanded before buying → reorders the trust blocks
- the deal-breakers → writes the FAQ
- the single best sentence from twelve parents → probably becomes the subhead

**Treat this deck as a placeholder that stops the page being empty during the sprint, not as
finished copy.** The finished copy is written on 19 September, out of the bank.
