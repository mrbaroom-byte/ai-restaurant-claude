## What is allowed to slip, and what is not

**Allowed to slip:** the launch date. A December launch that ships well beats a November launch
that ships badly, and no part of the plan breaks if 15 November moves.

**Not allowed to slip:** the cultural review, the acceptance test, and the dry run. Those three
are what the whole positioning rests on — a creative-learning brand rooted in Saudi Arabia that
ships an inaccurate sheet, a bleeding page, or a broken refund has lost the argument it was
making.
