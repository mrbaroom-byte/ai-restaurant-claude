# The product range — everything after the flagship

> **Why this document exists, and why it is not optional.**
>
> `03-economics/` shows the flagship cannot fund its own paid acquisition: the core pad at
> SAR 39 needs a 39% landing conversion to pay for a click. Every first-order lever — price,
> shipping, bundle — moves that number a little. Only one thing moves it decisively.
>
> **A second purchase from an existing customer carries no acquisition cost.** It converts at
> close to full contribution. So the range is not "what we do once the pad works" — the range
> *is* how the pad works. A single-SKU business shipping one low-priced physical item at a time
> is genuinely hard, and this document is the answer to that, not a wishlist.

---

## The one number that decides everything

**Repeat rate within 12 months.** Instrument it from the first order — email consent, cohort
tag, order-count field — rather than discovering in January that you never set it up.

From `range-model.xlsx`, cohort of 100 with a 30% sticker attach:

| Repeat rate | Contribution per acquired customer | Max CAC (keeping 30%) | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | SAR 17.80 | **12.46** | Single-transaction business. Gift positioning, and accept the CAC. |
| 10% | SAR 20.29 | **14.21** | Marginal. Paid acquisition still does not work. |
| 25% | SAR 24.03 | **16.82** | The range is working. Headroom up ~60% on the pad alone. |
| 40% | SAR 27.77 | **19.44** | Masterbrand thesis proved. Subscription becomes arguable. |

**An honest correction to the obvious reading of that table.** Even at 40% repeat, SAR 19.44 of
CAC headroom against a SAR 1.60 click still demands roughly **8% landing conversion** — better
than the flagship's 16%, and still above what cold traffic to an unknown brand usually delivers.

So the range does not make paid advertising work by itself. What it does:

- Nearly doubles acquisition headroom, from SAR 12.46 to SAR 19.44.
- Makes **retargeted and warm traffic** viable, where clicks are cheaper and convert harder.
- Makes the **activity kit** — SAR 59.76 contribution — the SKU that genuinely can carry cold
  paid traffic.

Nothing else in this document matters if repeat stays under 10%. Check it before funding wave two.

## Architecture — what is a series, what is a capsule, what is an extension

The brief's brand architecture, made operational:

| Layer | Role | Cadence | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Masterbrand** | Owns the promise and the trust | Permanent | Never carries a seasonal colour or an occasion |
| **Creative Journeys** رحلات إبداعية | The evergreen series. Repeat purchases live here. | 2–3 titles/year | Each title is a self-contained world; no title depends on owning another |
| **Seasonal capsules** | Captures event demand without defining the brand | 2/year max | Dated, limited, never restocked. Kept visually distinct from the series. |
| **Extensions** | Widens the basket and the age range | Opportunistic | Only launch one when it raises AOV or repeat rate, never for range's sake |
| **Institutional** | Different economics entirely | Quoted | Separate line, separate margins. Must not dilute the consumer masterbrand. |

**The discipline that matters:** the number-one risk in the brief is *patriotism without repeat
use* — traffic spikes at events, weak evergreen conversion. That risk is managed structurally,
by keeping capsules dated and separate, so a National Day spike never becomes the plan.

## Wave 1 — prove repeat (Nov 2026 → Jun 2027)

Everything here reuses artwork, editorial and tooling you have already paid for. That is the
point: wave one buys a second purchase at near-zero marginal content cost.

### 1.1 · Sticker sheets · ملصقات
**Launch: Jan 2027 · Test price SAR 15–19 · Attach, not standalone**

Two A5 sheets of stickers drawn from the twenty sheets' motifs — the oryx, the falcon, the
coffee pot, the roses, the reef fish, plus the completion seals.

- **Why first:** lowest COGS in the range, highest attach rate, and the artwork already exists.
  It is the cheapest possible test of whether a buyer will buy twice.
- **Job:** raises AOV on the flagship without raising shipping cost — stickers add grams, not a
  second parcel.
- **Watch:** if it adds pencils, crayons or any toy-classified item, SASO/SABER applies
  (`05-operations/compliance-checklist.md`). Paper stickers usually do not; **confirm before
  ordering.**

### 1.2 · Postcard set · بطاقات
**Launch: Feb 2027 · Test price SAR 25–29**

Twelve A6 cards, one side the line art to colour, the reverse laid out for writing and posting.

- **Why:** turns a colouring product into a *sent object*. A child who mails a card creates the
  most persuasive marketing you can't buy.
- **Job:** gifting occasion, and a lower price point for a first purchase from a hesitant parent.
- **Content:** reuses sheets 1, 2, 7, 15, 17 and the strongest capsule art.

### 1.3 · Creative Journeys, title two · رحلة الطبيعة
**Launch: Apr 2027 · Same format, same price as the flagship**

The second twenty-sheet pad. **Nature and animals of the Arabian Peninsula** — deliberately the
narrowest, most universal, least culturally-loaded subject in the world map below.

- **Why this subject second:** it is the one where the flagship's artwork already proves the
  approach works (the oryx, reef, falcon and Farasan sheets were the strongest in testing), and
  where cultural-accuracy risk is lowest while the illustrator relationship is still new.
- **Job:** the actual repeat-purchase test. Same buyer, same price, no discount. If the existing
  customer list does not convert on this, the range thesis is wrong and you should stop
  extending and fix the flagship.
- **Decision gate:** ≥20% of the flagship's buyer list purchases within 8 weeks, without a
  discount. Below that, do not fund wave two.

### 1.4 · Founding Day capsule · كبسولة يوم التأسيس
**Feb 2027 · Dated, limited, not restocked**

A short 8-sheet pad or a single premium poster sheet. Sold for three weeks, then gone.

- **Why limited:** this is the risk being managed, not indulged. A dated capsule captures the
  spike without teaching the market that the brand is an occasion brand.
- **Rule:** never discount the evergreen range alongside it, and never carry capsule artwork
  into a series title.

## Wave 2 — widen the basket and the age range (Jul 2027 → 2028)

Only if wave one clears its repeat gate.

### 2.1 · The activity kit · طقم الأنشطة
**Test price SAR 129–169**

Pad + stickers + quality colouring pencils + a display folder, in a rigid box designed to be
given.

- **Why it matters most:** `03-economics/` shows the bundle is the only offer whose contribution
  can carry a realistic CAC. A properly designed kit at SAR 149 is the first SKU where paid
  acquisition might actually work.
- **Blocker:** pencils are a toy-classified import. SASO/SABER conformity, supplier
  documentation, and the cost and lead time of that, must be settled **before** this is
  scheduled — not after.

### 2.2 · Ages 3–5 edition · إصدار الصغار
**Test price SAR 35–39**

Thicker line, fewer elements per page, no reading required, board-weight stock.

- **Why:** the brief's top-five risk list includes the too-broad age claim. The answer is
  age-tiered editions, not wider artwork. It also unlocks the younger sibling — the single
  easiest incremental sale in a children's category.

### 2.3 · Ages 9–12 edition · إصدار الكبار
**Test price SAR 45–49**

Higher detail density, longer discovery text, real map and diagram content.

- **Why:** answers "my older child called it babyish", and extends the customer's lifetime
  rather than finding a new customer.

### 2.4 · Creative Journeys, titles three and four
**Two per year thereafter**

Take the next two from the world map, in the order the customer list votes for with money.

## Wave 3 — the durable business (2028+)

These are hypotheses, not plans. Each needs its own validation sprint.

| | What | Why it could work | What would kill it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educator packs** | Classroom sets, 30 sheets + a teacher's guide, quoted per school | Different economics: no per-order shipping, high volume, no CAC | Procurement cycles are slow and margin pressure is severe. Never let it set consumer pricing. |
| **Subscription** | A pad every quarter | Solves the CAC problem permanently | Only viable above ~40% repeat. Below that it is a churn machine. |
| **Arabic-first digital companion** | Audio pronunciation of the discovery facts | Adds value without adding screen time — a parent-approved screen use | The brief bans anti-device messaging; do not position it as "good screens" |
| **Licensed editions** | A museum, a nature reserve, a heritage site | Distribution and credibility at once | Any partner named must be in writing (`claim-discipline.md`) |
| **Beyond Saudi Arabia** | GCC editions | The format travels; the content localises | Do not attempt until the Saudi repeat rate proves the format, not the patriotism |

## Economics of the range

Run `python3 business/06-roadmap/build_range_model.py` for the working model. The structure:

| SKU | Test price | Rough COGS | Contribution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sticker sheets | 17 | ~3 | **11.66** (attach — no shipping, no payment fee) | Raises AOV free |
| Postcards | 27 | ~7 | 12.05 | Entry price, gifting |
| Core pad | 39 / 49 | ~17.5 | 5.86 / **14.30** | The proposition |
| Ages 3–5 / 9–12 | 37 / 47 | ~17.5 | ~12–13 | Widens the household |
| Gift bundle | 89 | ~27 | 38.21 | The upsell |
| Activity kit | 149 | ~55 | **59.76** — the only one that carries cold paid traffic | What ads can afford |
| Educator pack | quoted | volume | different model entirely | Cash, not brand |

*Costs are placeholders pending quotes; the flagship figures match `03-economics/`.*

**Three rules the model enforces:**

1. **Attach beats acquire — but check the size of the effect.** A sticker sheet added to an
   existing order carries no shipping, no payment fee and no CAC, so it clears **SAR 11.66** of
   contribution on a SAR 17 price. Attaching to 30% of orders adds SAR 3.50 per order and costs
   nothing in conversion. Raising the pad from 39 to 49 adds more — SAR 8.45 — but costs
   conversion on every order. **Do the free one first, then test the priced one.** Do not read
   this as "attach beats a price rise"; it doesn't. It beats it on risk, not on size.
2. **Never discount the flagship to sell a new title.** It teaches the list to wait. Bundle
   instead — old title plus new title at a bundle price that still clears contribution.
3. **Each new SKU must clear its own tooling.** A title that needs SAR 20,000 of illustration
   must show a path to selling enough units to return it, at the *observed* repeat rate, not a
   hoped one.

## Sequencing — what gates what

```
Nov 2026   Flagship launches
           ↓  gate: 20-order dry run passed, first reviews are real
Jan 2027   Sticker sheets            (cheapest repeat test; artwork already paid for)
Feb 2027   Postcards + Founding Day capsule
           ↓  gate: any repeat purchase at all from the launch cohort
Apr 2027   Creative Journeys title 2 — Nature and Animals
           ↓  GATE: >=20% of flagship buyers purchase within 8 weeks, no discount
              FAILS -> stop extending. Fix the flagship or accept a gift business.
Jul 2027   Activity kit             (blocked on SASO/SABER for pencils)
Sep 2027   Ages 3-5 edition
Q4 2027    Title 3, second capsule
2028       Ages 9-12, titles 4-5, educator pilot
           ↓  gate: repeat rate >40% sustained
2029       Subscription
```

## What would make me wrong about all of this

Stated plainly, because a roadmap that cannot be falsified is decoration:

- **If the September sprint shows parents buy this as a gift, not for their own child**, the
  whole repeat-purchase thesis collapses and the right range is gifting-led: postcards, kits and
  capsules, with the pad as a component. Survey question E1 answers this before you build anything.
- **If the occasion data (E2) is dominated by National Day and Founding Day**, the evergreen
  series is the wrong bet and capsules are the business. That would be an unwelcome finding and
  a genuine one.
- **If illustration costs come in high enough** that each new title needs SAR 20,000+ of
  artwork, the range slows to one title a year and the extensions — stickers, postcards, kits,
  which reuse existing art — become the entire growth story.

Revisit this document after the 30 September gate review, and again after the first 200 orders.
It is a plan written before a single parent has been interviewed, and it should change.
